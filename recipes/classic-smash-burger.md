title: Classic Smash Burger
serves: 2
tags: [burger, beef, grill, dinner]
---

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup Hy-Vee unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp Hy-Vee Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 lb 90% lean Hy-Vee ground beef
- 2 slices Hy-Vee Cheddar cheese
- 2 Hy-Vee Bakery sesame seed hamburger buns
- 2 leaves of lettuce
- hamburger dill pickle slices
- red tomatoes
- red onion
- Hy-Vee ketchup

## Steps

1. Combine butter, Worcestershire sauce, and fish sauce in a medium bowl. Add ground beef; gently mix.
2. Shape into 4 meatballs. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, preheat a charcoal or gas grill for direct cooking over high heat.
4. Place a large cast-iron griddle on grill rack to preheat.
5. Remove meatballs from refrigerator; sprinkle with Hy-Vee kosher salt and Hy-Vee coarse-ground black pepper. Place meatballs 1 inch apart on hot griddle.
6. Sear for 30 seconds. Smash burgers to 1/4 inch thickness using a large spatula.
7. Turn burgers over; place 1 slice of cheese on top of each patty. Cook 1 to 2 minutes more or until cheese melts and burgers reach 160 degrees.
8. Butter and toast 2 split Hy-Vee Bakery sesame seed hamburger buns.
9. To assemble, place a lettuce leaf on each bun bottom, then top each with 2 burgers, pickle slices, red and yellow tomato slices, red onion, ketchup, and bun top.
