# Braised Chuck Roast with Mashed Potatoes and Gravy

A low-and-slow braised chuck roast scaled for a 1.79 lb roast, with Instant Pot mashed potatoes and a flour-thickened gravy made from the braising liquid. Serves about 3-4.

## Roast

### Ingredients

- 1.79 lb boneless chuck roast
- 1.5 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp oil (vegetable or avocado)
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1/2 tsp dried minced garlic
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 cup Cabernet
- About 1 cup unsalted beef broth (more on hand for topping up and extra gravy)
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary, crushed
- 1 bay leaf
- Baby carrots, about 12-15 (a good handful)

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 250°F, regular bake (not convection).
2. Pat the roast very dry. Season all over with the salt and pepper. No need to bring it to room temperature.
3. Heat a snug, oven-safe pot with a lid over medium-high. Add the oil and sear the roast 4-5 minutes per side until deeply browned. Move to a plate.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Cook the onion about 5 minutes, then add the garlic and tomato paste and stir about 1 minute until the paste darkens.
5. Deglaze with the Cabernet over a gentle simmer, scraping up the browned bits, until reduced by about half (3-5 minutes). Add the broth and Worcestershire and return to a simmer.
6. Set the roast back in the pot on top of the onions. Pour in any juices from the plate. Stir the thyme and rosemary into the liquid and add the bay leaf. The liquid should come about halfway up the roast.
7. Cover and braise at 250°F for about 3-4 hours. Check the liquid around the 2-hour mark and top up with a splash of broth if low.
8. Add the baby carrots for the last hour or so, then keep cooking until the roast shreds with almost no resistance to a fork.
9. Rest the roast about 15 minutes. Strain the braising liquid, skim the fat, and discard the bay leaf.

## Mashed Potatoes (Instant Pot)

### Ingredients

- 24 oz honey gold bite-sized potatoes
- 1 cup water and 1 tsp salt (for the Instant Pot)
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup buttermilk, warmed
- About 3/4 tsp salt, to taste
- 1/8 tsp white pepper

### Method

1. Cook the potatoes with the water and 1 tsp salt in the Instant Pot for 5 minutes on high pressure, then quick release.
2. Drain well and let any steam dry off in the hot pot for a minute.
3. Add the butter and mash right away. Pour in the warm buttermilk a little at a time until creamy. Season with salt and white pepper.

## Gravy

### Ingredients

- The strained braising liquid from the pot
- 2 tbsp flour whisked into 1/4 cup cold water
- Salt to taste

### Method

1. Bring the braising liquid to a gentle simmer on the stove (small bubbles across roughly a quarter to a third of the surface).
2. Whisk the flour and cold water until completely smooth. Pour in slowly while whisking constantly.
3. Whisk constantly for the first minute, then drop to medium-low and simmer 3-5 minutes, stirring every 30 seconds or so, until it coats the back of a spoon and doesn't taste floury.
4. Taste and add salt a pinch at a time. The broth is unsalted, so it will likely need some.

## Notes

- Timing: the roast goes in the oven about 30 minutes after you start searing. Start the potatoes roughly 30 minutes before the roast is done.
- Lower and slower is better than hotter. Chuck is tender once it reaches about 200-205°F internally, and going well past that just dries it out.
- Salt: the unsalted broth gave room for 1.5 tsp salt on the roast. Salted broth would call for closer to 1 tsp.
- Leftovers: reheat the roast in a 300°F oven with the lid on and a splash of broth, 25-30 minutes. Warm the mash and gravy separately.
- Dogs: onions and garlic are toxic to dogs, so keep scraps away from pets.
