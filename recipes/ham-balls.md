title: Ham Balls
serves: 24
tags: [ham, dinner, meat, oven]
---

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs hamburger
- 3 lbs ground ham
- 3 eggs
- 3 cups cracker crumbs
- 2 cups milk
- 2 cups tomato soup, for the sauce
- 1/2 cup vinegar, for the sauce
- 2 cups brown sugar, for the sauce
- 1 tablespoon dry mustard, for the sauce

## Steps

1. Make into balls, 3/4 cup each. Make sauce and pour over balls.
2. Bake 1 hour at 350 degrees.
3. Bake 1 1/2 hours if frozen.

## Notes

The first five ingredients make the ham balls, and the last four make the sauce. Cook time 1 hour. Bake 1 1/2 hours if frozen. Serves is an estimate; the card gave no yield.
