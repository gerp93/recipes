## Ingredients

- 15 ounces crushed fire roasted tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1 small clove garlic, 1/2 medium
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- Scant 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

## Steps

1. Cut the garlic into a few rough pieces, no need to mince it.
2. Place all ingredients in a blender. Blend until fully combined.

## Notes

From acouplecooks.com. Uses only 5 ingredients and 5 minutes in a blender. Makes about 1 1/2 cups. This sauce is Italian-style, with a slightly runnier texture than American style pizza sauce. Serves is an estimate for a batch of sauce.
