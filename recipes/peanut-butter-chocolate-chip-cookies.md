## Ingredients

- 1 Cup Margarin or Butter
- 1 cup Peanut Butter
- 1 Cup Sugar
- 1 Cup Brown Sugar
- 2 Eggs
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 2 Cups Flour
- Salt
- 1 tap soda
- 1 Cup Chocolate Chips

## Steps

1. Mix in order. Dough will be soft.
2. Make into balls and dip in sugar.
3. Bake 10-12 min @ 350 degrees.
