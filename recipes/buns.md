## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk, scalded
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 stick oleo
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 2 packages yeast
- 1 tsp salt
- flour, enough to make thick

## Steps

1. Mix the sugar, oleo, eggs, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Add hot water to make it thinner.
3. Add sugar and salt to the yeast and let it stand.
4. After the milk boils, add it to the mixture.

## Notes
