## Ingredients

- 1 egg plus enough water to equal 3/4 cup plus 1 Tbs
- 1 Tbs oil
- 4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups flour
- 3 tsp dill weed
- 2 tsp dehydrated onion
- 2 tsp active dry yeast

## Steps

1. Mix ingredients in order.
2. Form into loaf.
3. Place in greased bread pan and let raise.
4. Bake at 350 degrees for x minutes.

## Notes

Adapted from a bread machine recipe. Makes a 1 lb loaf. Prep 20 minutes. The card shows the bake time as x, so the bake time is not known. Serves is an estimate for one loaf.
