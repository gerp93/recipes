## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup oil
- 3 cups bread flour
- 2 1/2 tsp yeast

## Steps

1. Combine the ingredients in the order listed and mix into a dough.
2. Form the dough into breadsticks.
3. Bake at 350°F until golden brown.
