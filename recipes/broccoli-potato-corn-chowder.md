---
title: Broccoli, Potato, Corn Chowder
serves: 6
tags: [soup, chowder, broccoli, potato]
---

## Ingredients

- butter
- onion
- potato, peeled and diced raw
- seasonings
- water, enough to cover
- broccoli
- 1/2 bag frozen corn
- milk, cream, or half and half
- grated cheese

## Steps

1. Heat butter over med. high heat in Dutch oven. When sizzling add onion and cook a minute or two.
2. Add peeled, diced raw potato and seasonings, stir for a minute and then add enough water to cover. Simmer until potatoes are fork tender.
3. Meanwhile, chop broccoli and lightly steam. I did this in the microwave.
4. When potatoes are tender, add the steamed broccoli and 1/2 bag of frozen corn. Continue to simmer gently.
5. Slowly add milk/cream/half and half while stirring constantly. Check seasonings and add more if necessary. It should be flavorful, not bland.
6. Lower heat and add about half of the grated cheese, stirring gently until melted and incorporated into the chowder.
7. Serve with more cheese sprinkled on top. I served it with a tossed green salad and cornbread.
