---
title: Imperial Bulldog
serves: 1
tags: [cocktail, underberg, tiki]
---

## Ingredients

- 3/4 oz aquavit
- 3/4 oz cachaça
- 3/4 oz lime juice
- 3/4 oz pineapple juice
- 3/4 oz simple syrup
- 1 bottle Underberg Bitters
- red raspberries

## Steps

1. Muddle 5 raspberries in the bottom of a chilled pilsner or Belgian beer glass and add crushed ice.
2. Combine the aquavit, cachaça, lime juice, pineapple juice, and simple syrup in a cocktail shaker with ice and shake until chilled.
3. Pour into the glass and add more crushed ice.
4. Garnish with pineapple leaves, red raspberries, and an inverted bottle of Underberg.
