---
title: Grilled Broccoli with Lemon & Parmesan
serves: 4
tags: [vegetable, broccoli, grill, side-dish]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb broccoli florets, roughly 8 cups
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 lemon, sliced
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, shredded

## Steps

1. Heat grill to medium heat, 350°F.
2. Toss broccoli florets with olive oil, lemon juice and lemon slices. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Grill broccoli for 7-10 minutes, stirring every couple of minutes. I recommend using a vegetable grilling plate. Broccoli is done when it is bright green with some crispy, brown bits.
4. Transfer broccoli to serving plate, sprinkle with parmesan cheese and serve.

## Notes

From sweetpeasandsaffron.com. Best served right after cooking, but you can pre-chop the broccoli florets and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. Serves is an estimate; the card gave no yield or times.
