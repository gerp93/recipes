---

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups shortening
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 pinch salt

## Steps

1. Beat together the vinegar, egg, and water.
2. Combine the shortening, flour, and salt.
3. Mix the egg mixture into the flour mixture.

## Notes

From Mom. Serves one 8 inch pie. Makes 3 bottoms and 2 tops.
