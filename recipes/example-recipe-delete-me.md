## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 slices bread

## Steps

1. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
2. Crack the eggs into the pan and add the salt.
3. Cook for 3 minutes, then flip and cook 1 more minute.
4. Toast the bread and serve the eggs on top.
