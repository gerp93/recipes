---
title: Alpine Sour
serves: 1
tags: [cocktail, underberg, sour]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 oz Asbach Uralt
- 1 oz orgeat
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1 bottle Underberg bitters
- grated nutmeg

## Steps

1. Shake all ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe glass.
2. Garnish with grated nutmeg.

## Notes

By Damon Boelte, Prime Meats, New York. Uses Asbach Uralt, a German style brandy with notes of raisin and spice. From thehumblegarnish.com.
