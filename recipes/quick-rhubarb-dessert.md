title: Quick Rhubarb Dessert
serves: 10
total_time: 1h 5m
tags: [dessert, rhubarb, oven, easy]
---

## Ingredients

- 4 Cups Cut Rhubarb
- 1 Cup Sugar
- 1 package Strawberry Jello, 3 oz.
- 1 Dry Yellow Cake Mix
- 1 Cup Water
- 1/3 Cup Melted Butter or Oleo

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
2. Put in 9x13 pan in order given. Do not mix.
3. Bake 1 hour and serve with whipped cream.

## Notes

Prep 5 minutes, cook 1 hour. Layer in the pan in the order the ingredients are listed.
