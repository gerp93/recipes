title: Corrections
serves: 1
tags: [cocktail, underberg, amaro]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 bottle Underberg Bitters
- 1 oz Unicum
- 1 oz Cynar
- 1/2 oz Fernet Branca

## Steps

1. Stir with ice in a mixing glass until chilled.
2. Strain and serve in a rocks glass with a large cube.
3. Garnish with an expressed lemon peel.

## Notes

By Toby Maloney. A herbaceous amaro cocktail. From thehumblegarnish.com.
