## Ingredients

- 2 cans frozen limeade concentrate, 12 fluid ounces each
- 1 bottle lemon-lime flavored carbonated beverage, 2 liter
- 1 jar maraschino cherries, 10 ounce, drained and juice reserved
- 1 lime

## Steps

1. Pour both cans of concentrated limeade into a large pitcher.
2. Mix in lemon-lime soda. Stir in reserved cherry juice.
3. Squeeze juice from lime into mixture, then slice and set aside. Stir well and serve over ice.
4. Garnish with cherries and lime slices.

## Notes

From Allrecipes.com. Extra tart cherry limeade. To reduce the tartness, stir in an additional bottle of lemon-lime soda. Prep 5 minutes. Serves is an estimate; the card gave no yield.
