## Ingredients

- 1 cup shortening
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Blend together shortening and peanut butter until smooth.
2. Add sugar gradually, beating until creamy.
3. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
4. Add vanilla, sift together flour, soda, baking powder, and salt and blend in mixture. Mix until smooth.
5. Drop by tsp on greased cookie sheet.
6. Bake at 375 degrees for 8-10 minutes.

## Notes

Makes 6-8 dozen. Prep 20 minutes, cook 8-10 minutes per pan, total 60-100 minutes. Saved as number 2 because a Peanut Butter Cookies recipe was already in the box. Serves is the midpoint of 6-8 dozen.
