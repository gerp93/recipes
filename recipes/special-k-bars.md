# Special K Bars

**From:** Mom
**Serves:** not listed on the card

## Ingredients

- 2 c. white sugar
- 2 c. white syrup
- 24 oz. jar peanut butter
- 12 cups Special K cereal
- 1 pkg. chocolate chips
- 1 pkg. butterscotch chips

## Instructions

1. Combine the sugar and white syrup and bring to a boil.
2. Add the 24 oz. jar of peanut butter. This will melt.
3. Pour over the 12 cups of Special K cereal.
4. Place in a greased jelly roll pan and press down.
5. Melt the chocolate chips and butterscotch chips together.
6. Pour over the top and cut immediately.

## Notes

Transcribed from Mom's handwritten recipe card (The Longaberger Company, 1994).
