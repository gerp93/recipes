title: Easy Sangria Slushies
serves: 8
tags: [drink, sangria, slushie, frozen, party]
---

## Ingredients

- 750ml bottle Red Moscato
- 1 cup raspberries
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1/4 cup Cointreau or Triple Sec
- 1/4 cup Chambord or Blackberry Brandy
- 1/4 cup fresh squeezed orange juice
- 2 tablespoons simple syrup

## Steps

1. Add all ingredients to a blender and pulse until blended.
2. Pour the sangria into a 9x13-inch pan and place in the freezer for 2 hours.
3. Break up semi-frozen drink into a slushie and serve with a straw.
