title: Authentic German Spaetzle
serves: 6
total_time: 35m

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour, or whole wheat flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 4 large eggs
- 1/2 cup milk or water, plus more as needed. Milk produces a richer spaetzle. Add more flour if the dough is too runny, more milk or water if it's too stiff
- butter for serving

## Steps

1. Add the flour and salt to the bowl of a stand mixer. Stir to combine.
2. Crack the eggs into a small bowl and whisk them.
3. Make a well in the center of the flour mixture and pour the eggs in it. Add the milk, starting with slightly less and adding more as needed.
4. Attach a dough hook to the stand mixer and knead the dough for 16-20 minutes, or until bubbles appear. After 15 minutes or less of beating, use a wooden spoon to scoop and pull the dough. If bubbles or holes appear, the dough is done.
5. Bring at least 2 quarts of lightly salted water to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
6. Using a spaetzle maker of your choice, press the noodles into the simmering water and cook for about 2-3 minutes, or until the noodles float to the top.
7. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the noodles to a colander, and then dump the noodles in a large bowl of ice water.
8. Drain the noodles again, toss with some melted butter and serve warm.

## Notes

From daringgourmet.com. Authentic German spaetzle from the Swabia region of Southern Germany. Prep 25m, cook 10m. Make ahead: store in the fridge for a couple of days, then reheat by tossing in melted butter in a large skillet.
