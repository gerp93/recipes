title: Mantequilla
serves: 1
tags: [cocktail, underberg, tiki]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 1/3 oz Angostura Rum 1919
- 1/3 oz Four Roses bourbon
- 1 oz Skinos mastiha spirit
- 1 1/3 oz pineapple juice
- 1/3 oz passion fruit syrup
- 1/2 oz fresh lime juice
- 1 barspoon Underberg bitters

## Steps

1. Combine the ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice and shake until chilled.
2. Strain into a tiki mug filled with crushed ice.
3. Garnish with a pineapple ring and a few more dashes of Underberg.

## Notes

By Spyros Patsialos. Entered in a Skinos cocktail competition, and the most complex of the Underberg recipes. Skinos is a Greek spirit made with the resin of the Mastiha tree, so it may be hard to find. Liber and Co. passion fruit syrup is recommended. From thehumblegarnish.com.
