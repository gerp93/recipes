serves: 8

## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk, scalded
- 1/4 cup Crisco
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 cups flour

## Steps

1. Heat the milk until scalded.
2. Put the Crisco and butter in a big bowl.
3. Add the sugar and salt to the bowl.
4. Pour in the hot milk to melt the shortening, then stir.
5. Add the flour.

## Notes

From Grandma and Grandma Mary. Single batch. The card stops after adding the flour. Serves count is an estimate, not on the card.
