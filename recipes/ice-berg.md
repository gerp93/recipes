---
title: Ice-Berg
serves: 1
tags: [cocktail, underberg, blended]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 oz reposado tequila
- 1 oz orgeat
- 1 bottle Underberg Bitters
- 3/4 oz lemon juice
- 1 cup crushed ice

## Steps

1. Combine the ingredients in a blender with the crushed ice.
2. Blend until fluffy.
3. Garnish with an empty Underberg bottle.

## Notes

By Garret Richard. Basically an Underberg slushie. From thehumblegarnish.com.
