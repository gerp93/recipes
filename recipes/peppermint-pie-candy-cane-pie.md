## Ingredients

- 1 Oreo pie crust
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 2 packages cream cheese, 8-ounce each, softened
- 10 drops red food coloring
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp peppermint extract
- 1/2 cup peppermint candy canes or mints, crushed
- 1 tub Cool Whip
- Crushed peppermint candy for decoration

## Steps

1. In a large bowl whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form. Set aside.
2. In a medium bowl cream together the powdered sugar and cream cheese.
3. Add the food coloring and vanilla to the cream cheese and powdered sugar bowl and mix until a uniform color.
4. Mix in the 1/2 cup of peppermint candy bits with the cream cheese and powdered sugar.
5. Add the powdered sugar and cream cheese mixture to the bowl of heavy whipping cream and mix together on low speed until combined.
6. Pour mixture into Oreo crust.
7. Top pie with Cool Whip and crushed peppermint. Chill for 1-2 hours or until ready to serve.

## Notes

From princesspinkygirl.com. Also called Candy Cane Pie. Makes 8 slices. Prep 15 minutes. Chill 1-2 hours before serving, which is not counted in the total time.
