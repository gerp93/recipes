## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Kraft mayonnaise, don't use Dukes or Miracle Whip!
- 1/4 cup mustard
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 tablespoon rice wine vinegar, leave out if using for dip
- 1 dash salt
- cayenne pepper

## Steps

1. Whisk together and chill.
