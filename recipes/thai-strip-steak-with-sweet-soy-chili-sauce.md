title: Thai Strip Steak with Sweet Soy Chili Sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 New York strip steak, about 1.08 lb
- 6 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1.5 tbsp lime juice
- 1.5 tbsp fish sauce
- 1.5 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic, grated
- 1.5 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 red Fresno chili, thinly sliced, plus extra for garnish
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 scallions, chopped
- 1 tbsp high-heat oil for searing

## Steps

1. Whisk the soy sauce, oyster sauce, brown sugar, lime juice, fish sauce, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and sliced chili together.
2. Reserve half of the sauce for drizzling at the end.
3. Marinate the steak in the other half for 30 to 60 minutes at room temperature.
4. Heat the oven to 250 degrees Fahrenheit.
5. Pat the marinade off the steak so the sugars do not burn.
6. Set the steak on a rack over a sheet pan and roast until it reaches 115 to 120 degrees inside, about 30 to 45 minutes.
7. Heat a skillet, ideally cast iron, until very hot with a thin film of high-heat oil.
8. Sear the steak 60 to 90 seconds per side until crusty and caramelized, watching closely because the sugars brown fast.
9. Rest the steak for 5 to 8 minutes.
10. Slice against the grain into half-inch strips.
11. Drizzle with the reserved sauce and top with Fresno chili rings, scallions, and sesame seeds.

## Notes

Reconstructed Thai Steak inspired by CRAVE, not their exact recipe. Marinade amounts are scaled 1.5 times for a 1.08 lb strip. Serve with sticky rice, pressed and pan-fried until crispy if you like, and stir-fried vegetables such as snap peas, bell peppers, broccolini, and carrots. For a pan sear only, skip the oven and sear 3 to 4 minutes per side to 130 to 135 degrees.
