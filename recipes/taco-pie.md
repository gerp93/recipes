## Ingredients

- 1 lb. Hamburger
- Onion to taste
- 1 package crescent rolls
- 1 package taco seasoning mix
- Tortilla chips
- 1 Cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup mozzarella cheese
- 1/4 cup cheddar cheese

## Steps

1. Brown hamburger and onion; drain.
2. Add taco seasoning and prepare according to package directions. Add sour cream to hamburger mixture.
3. Place crescent rolls on bottom of pie plate or round cake pan as crust.
4. Crush chips and put over crust.
5. Pour hamburger mixture on top of chips. Add cheeses.
6. Crush more chips and put over top.
7. Bake at 350 degrees approximately 20 to 25 minutes. Serve with taco sauce.

## Notes

From the Rake Mange Takk cook book, Tami Jacobson. Prep 20 minutes, cook 20 minutes. Serve with taco sauce.
