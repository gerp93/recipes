## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1 pinch salt
- 1/3 to 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp butter for the pan

## Steps

1. Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, mix the buttermilk, egg, and melted butter, starting with 1/3 cup of buttermilk.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir just until combined, adding more buttermilk if the batter is too thick.
4. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
5. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.
6. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter into the pan for each pancake.
7. Flip when bubbles form and pop on the surface and the edges look set, about 2 to 3 minutes.
8. Cook another 1 to 2 minutes on the second side until golden.
9. Serve right away.

## Notes

Makes about 3 medium pancakes. Lumps in the batter are fine; overmixing makes them tough. A single-serving alternative to the 4-serving Buttermilk Pancakes recipes.
