---
title: North Dakota Caramel Rolls
serves: 14
tags: [rolls, caramel, breakfast, yeast, north-dakota]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 pkg rapid rise active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 6 cups bread flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 3 cups brown sugar
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons light corn syrup
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 3 tablespoons white sugar

## Steps

1. Dissolve yeast in 1/2 cup of water.
2. In a large standing mixer, cream sugar and shortening. Add buttermilk, yeast mixture, 2 cups flour, salt, baking powder and soda. Beat until smooth.
3. Stir in remaining 4 cups of flour to make a very soft dough. I did this part with my mixer's bread hook. Turn onto a floured surface and knead 4-8 minutes or until the dough is smooth and elastic.
4. Let the dough rest for 10 minutes while you make the caramel sauce.
5. Combine brown sugar, cream and light corn syrup in a saucepan. Heat over medium heat until sugar is dissolved and sauce is slightly thickened.
6. Pour caramel sauce in equal amounts in the bottom of two 9x13 pans. I usually get 12-14 rolls out of one batch. In order to avoid crowding, don't place more than 6-7 rolls in each pan.
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll dough into a large 10x18 inch rectangle. Spread with the softened butter. Sprinkle cinnamon and sugar over the dough.
8. Starting with the long side, roll up and press to seal. Cut into 1 inch thick slices.
9. Place the rolls on top of the caramel sauce in your 2 pans. Cover with a bread cloth and let rise near your warming oven until doubled in size, about 1-2 hours.
10. Bake in preheated 375° oven for 25-30 minutes. Invert onto platter while rolls are still warm.

## Notes

From bigoven.com. The brown sugar, heavy cream and corn syrup are for the caramel sauce. The butter, cinnamon and white sugar are for the filling. Makes 12-14 rolls from one batch, split between two 9x13 pans. Serves is an estimate. The card gave no times.
