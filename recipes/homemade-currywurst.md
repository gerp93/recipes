## Ingredients

- Bratwursts
- 1 cup of Tomato Ketchup
- 1/4 teaspoon of Baking Soda, sounds weird but trust me
- 4 to 5 teaspoons of Mild Curry Powder
- 2 teaspoons of Smoked Paprika
- 1 teaspoon of Onion Powder
- 1/8 to 1/4 teaspoon of Cayenne Pepper
- 2 teaspoons of Worcestershire Sauce
- 4 tablespoons of Beef Broth
- 2 or more tablespoons of Water

## Steps

1. Put 1 cup of tomato ketchup in a small saucepan and heat on medium low. When ketchup is warmed through, add baking soda and stir continuously until foaming subsides. Ketchup should look normal again.
2. Reduce heat to low. Add remaining ingredients, through to water and stir until combined. Let it simmer on the stove for 5 to 10 minutes to meld flavours.
3. Serve warm over sliced bratwurst with crusty bread or fries as a side.

## Notes

From thekitchenmaus.com. A quick and easy currywurst that brings back memories of Germany. Smoky, spicy and a little sweet, and perfect over bratwurst. Prep 5m, cook 15m. Serves is an estimate; the card gave no yield.
