## Ingredients

- 1 cup shortening
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 1/4 cups brown sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Blend the shortening and peanut butter until smooth.
3. Add the white and brown sugar gradually, beating until creamy.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. Add the vanilla.
6. Sift together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
7. Blend the flour mixture into the creamy mixture until smooth.
8. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto a cookie sheet, greased lightly the first time only.
9. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes.

## Notes

Makes about 8 dozen cookies. From Mrs. Ivan Fair.
