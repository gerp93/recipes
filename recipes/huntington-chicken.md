## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked chicken
- 2 cups broth
- 1 cup cream
- 4 tbsp flour
- noodles, amount not given
- 1 cup grated cheese
- pimentos, amount not given

## Steps

1. Thicken the broth and cream with the flour.
2. Add the chicken and noodles.
3. Top with the grated cheese and pimentos.
4. Bake until golden brown on top.

## Notes

From Grandma Klipping. The card gives no amount for the noodles or pimentos, no oven temperature, and no bake time. Serves count is an estimate, not on the card.
