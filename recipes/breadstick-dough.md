---
title: Breadstick Dough
serves: 18
tags: [bread, breadsticks, yeast, pizza, appetizer]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup oil
- 3 cups flour
- 2 1/2 teaspoons yeast

## Steps

1. Add yeast to water and let dissolve.
2. Combine yeast mixture with remaining ingredients.
3. Form into breadsticks, or other desired shape, and bake at 350 for 10-15 minutes.

## Notes

Makes 18 breadsticks. Prep 25 minutes, cook 10-15 minutes, total 35-40 minutes.
