---
title: The Influence
serves: 1
tags: [cocktail, underberg, old-fashioned]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 bottle Underberg Bitters
- 1 oz Wild Turkey 101 Rye, or other overproof rye like Rittenhouse or Old Overholt
- 1 bar spoon demerara syrup
- 9 drops Angostura bitters
- 9 drops orange bitters

## Steps

1. Stir with ice in a mixing glass until chilled.
2. Strain and serve in a rocks glass with a large cube.
3. Garnish with an expressed orange peel.

## Notes

By Tyler Fry, The Violet Hour. An Underberg-centric old fashioned. If you don't store bitters in droppers, try 1 to 2 shakes of each type of bitters and add more if needed. From thehumblegarnish.com.
