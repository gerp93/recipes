## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup butter or margarine
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 3 - 4 browning bananas, mashed
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup buttermilk or sour milk, or sub 1 Tbs white vinegar plus enough milk to equal a half cup
- 1/2 cup broken nuts, if desired

## Steps

1. Cream butter and sugar well.
2. Add bananas, eggs, and vanilla.
3. Sift together flour, soda, and salt.
4. Add the dry ingredients alternately with the buttermilk.
5. Add the nuts.
6. Pour into a 9x5x3 inch greased loaf pan.
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 1 1/4 hours.

## Notes

From Trinity Cookbook, Delicious Banana Bread, Mrs. Swan Peterson, page 51. Makes 1 loaf. Prep 20 minutes, cook 75 minutes. Serves is an estimate for one loaf.
