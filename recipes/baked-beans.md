---
title: Baked Beans
serves: 10
tags: [beans, side-dish, baked, trinity-cookbook]
---

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs beans, Navy or Pinto
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1/2 cup dark molasses
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 7 or more strips of bacon, cut up
- 1 1/2 tsp dry mustard
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1/2 cup Ketchup

## Steps

1. Soak beans overnight in cold water.
2. Drain and cover with fresh water and simmer gently until skin start to burst.
3. Mix molasses, ketchup, salt, pepper, mustard, and sugar and pour over beans.
4. Add bacon and onion. Liquid should cover beans; add more water as needed.
5. Bake in slow oven at 300° for 6 to 8 hours; remove cover for last hour.

## Notes

From Trinity cookbook. Mrs. Joseph M. Johnson, page 141. Serves is an estimate; the card gave no yield.
