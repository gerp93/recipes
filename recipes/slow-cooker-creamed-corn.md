---
title: Slow Cooker Creamed Corn
serves: 8
tags: [side-dish, corn, slow-cooker, thanksgiving, make-ahead]
---

## Ingredients

- 2 lb frozen corn
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3/4 cup whipping cream
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Put the frozen corn in the cold insert with the unit off.
2. Add the butter, whipping cream, sugar, kosher salt and black pepper on top.
3. Set the cooker to 200 degrees and put the lid on.
4. Wait 20 to 30 minutes, then stir to break up the frozen block and mix in the butter and cream.
5. Stir about every 30 minutes until the corn is hot and creamy.
6. Switch to warm until serving.

## Notes

Made in a Presto multi-cooker with a ceramic insert. Start with everything cold so the ceramic does not crack from sudden heat. The sugar is optional. Takes about 2 to 3 hours at 200 degrees. Keep the heat at 200 or lower so the cream does not scorch.
