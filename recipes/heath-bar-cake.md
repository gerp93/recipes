title: Heath Bar Cake
serves: 12
tags: [cake, dessert, chocolate, family-recipe]
---

## Ingredients

- 1 box German chocolate cake mix
- 2 cups water
- 1 package 3 oz vanilla instant pudding
- 2 egg whites, beaten
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup butter
- 2 egg yolks
- 8 oz Cool Whip
- Heath candy bits, amount not given

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix the cake mix, water, vanilla pudding, and beaten egg whites together.
3. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, then let the cake cool.
4. For the frosting, beat the powdered sugar, butter, and egg yolks together.
5. Fold the frosting into the Cool Whip.
6. Spread the frosting over the cooled cake.
7. Top with Heath candy bits.
8. Refrigerate.
